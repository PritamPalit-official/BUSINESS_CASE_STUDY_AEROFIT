# BUSINESS_CASE_STUDY_AEROFIT
Customer Profiling &amp; Purchase Behavior Analysis for Aerofit Treadmills

Project Overview
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior for Aerofit treadmill products using data
analytics techniques. The goal is to understand how demographic and fitness-related attributes
influence product selection and to provide actionable insights for marketing, pricing, and product
strategy.
Business Problem
Aerofit aims to improve its marketing efficiency and product recommendations by identifying distinct
customer profiles for its three treadmill models (KP281, KP481, KP781). The analysis focuses on
understanding customer segments based on age, income, gender, fitness level, and usage
behavior.
Dataset Description
The dataset contains 180 customer records with 9 features, including age, gender, marital status,
income, fitness rating, usage frequency, miles run, and purchased treadmill model. The dataset is
complete with no missing values and includes both numerical and categorical variables.
Analysis Performed
• Exploratory Data Analysis: Data structure, distributions, and summary statistics.
• Non-Graphical Analysis: Grouped statistics and contingency tables.
• Univariate Analysis: Age and income distributions across customer segments.
• Bivariate Analysis: Product vs income, usage frequency, and fitness level.
• Probability Analysis: Marginal and conditional probabilities for product purchase behavior.
• Correlation Analysis: Relationships between income, usage, fitness, and product selection.
Tools & Technologies
• Python
• Pandas, NumPy
• Matplotlib, Seaborn
• Jupyter Notebook
Key Business Insights
The analysis reveals clear customer segmentation across product lines. Entry-level models attract
younger, budget-conscious customers, while premium models are preferred by high-income and
fitness-oriented users. Income, usage frequency, and fitness level show strong influence on
treadmill choice, enabling precise targeted marketing strategies.
Strategic Recommendations
• Target high-income, fitness-focused customers for premium models using professional platforms.
• Promote entry-level treadmills through affordability-focused campaigns.
• Introduce upgrade and trade-in offers to move customers up the product ladder.
• Bundle products and offer EMI options to increase conversion rates.
How to Use This Repository
This repository serves as a portfolio-quality business analytics project suitable for interviews,
academic evaluation, and practical demonstrations of customer segmentation and data-driven
